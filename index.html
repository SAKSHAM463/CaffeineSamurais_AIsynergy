01 The core problem

Two failures that feed each other

१

Exposure

Tansen Samaroh is famous — lots of people know about it and a good number attend. Smaller events in the same city are often just as good, but nobody hears about them. Small operators depend on word-of-mouth and social media.

  • 67% of visits at two attractions
  • Listings scattered across sites & social
vicious
loop
२

Availability

When Tansen Samaroh is sold out or too crowded, the visitor has no second option. There's no single place to see what's on today, tomorrow or next week — so they leave, and the small events stay invisible.

  • 5–8 hour day visits
  • No fallback when the headline is full

08 Verified guides & craftspeople

Qualified for this site. Accountable on the day.

A tourist doesn't need to know a person once produced a document. They need two checks — and neither requires storing a single Aadhaar number.

Check 1 · Qualified

Credential confirmed with the issuing body (tourism board, artisan registry) — mapped to the specific sites they may guide.

Check 2 · Accountable

Masked platform contact, trip sharing, ratings tied to completed tours, and a working complaint channel.

We do not collect

Aadhaar, PAN or driving-licence copies. Holding them is legally constrained (2018 Supreme Court ruling) and adds risk, not safety.

11 SRS & implementation plan

What we build, in what order

Functional requirements

FR-01

Event board filtered by today / tomorrow / week, category and distance from the fort corridor.

FR-02

When an event is marked full, show ≥3 alternatives within the visitor's remaining time window.

FR-03

Every listing carries a provenance label: verified / stakeholder-provided / inferred, with last-checked date.

FR-04

Stale data (> 7 days unchecked) is visibly flagged and demoted — never silently shown as current.

FR-05

Guide listings show site-specific qualification and accountability, never ID documents.

FR-06

Sensitive and fragile locations show local norms and are subject to promotion caps.

FR-07

Phone login with full international country-code support; browsing works without login.

Non-functional

NFR

Mobile-first, < 2s on 3G, bilingual Hindi/English, DPDP-Act-aligned data minimisation.

Phase 1 · Weeks 1–2

Event board + provenance

Schema, operator submission form, verification queue, today/tomorrow/week views.

Phase 2 · Weeks 3–4

Plan-B engine

"Full" signal, time-window filter, distance ranking, maps deep-links.

Phase 3 · Weeks 5–6

Guides & craftspeople

Issuer-confirmed credentials, masked contact relay, ratings tied to tours.

Phase 4 · Weeks 7–8

Crowd-aware promotion

Visitor caps per fragile site, rotation, resident feedback channel.

Pilot

Measure the spread

Target: share of visits outside the top-2 attractions rises from 33% → 45%.
